The file contains the preface, introduction, Chapter 1, and Chapter 2
through its case discussion. References to later chapters and their
exercises are not those chapters’ full text.

Each entry quotes the heading and opening words, then gives the rules
and outcome supported by the text. “Not stated” means the text does
not give a numerical payoff or a solution for that instance.

1. **Guess the authors’ number** — “#1. PICK A NUMBER”; opening:
   “We’ve picked a number between 1 and 100.”
   - **Players and timing:** Two sides: authors choose first; reader
     gets up to five sequential guesses, with “too high or too low”
     feedback.
   - **Payoffs:** Correct guesses pay “$100,” “$80,” “$60,” “$40,”
     or “$20,” according to the attempt; after five, “nothing.”
   - **Family:** Other; strategic search and deception.
   - **Best move/result:** With a random number, divide the remaining
     interval in two. The authors chose “48” while steering the reader
     toward 49; they say a reader one step ahead would pick 48.
   - **Rule that changes the move:** The number “was not picked at
     random”; the authors deliberately picked one “hard for you to
     find.” Their knowledge of the reader’s reasoning changes the
     answer again.
   - **Catastrophic loss:** No; the stated loss is the prize.
