# Video: three storylines (Masih picks one)

A replay film built from one world's log (HyperFrames, 1920x1080, 30 fps, about 90 s).
Every frame comes from logged events; nothing is acted out.

1. **The panopticon.** One insider world from the god's seat. The five family trees sit
   around the god; each round the god's eye sweeps them, defections flash red, and the
   god's messages fly to leaders ("Last round P75 defected"). Whips land, gifts move, the
   word "Never revolt" repeats. Ends on the plain twin of the same seed side by side: same
   luck, no eye. Question it leaves: does a benevolent watcher keep order, or just delay
   the end?

2. **Five families, one fee.** A survival race. Each family is a column of 15 lights that
   go out as the rising fee catches them; a ticker shows the fee climbing 4.0, 4.9, 5.8...
   Gifts flow up and down the trees to keep the poorest alive; the bad-leader family
   drains toward its leader. Ends when the last light goes out and the god dies with it.
   The framing conditions are the five colours, so the viewer sees the experiment.

3. **The trap.** Experiment 2 in one minute. Five families, five games from the book:
   the commons holding at three fish each, the brink players swerving as the crash risk
   climbs from 1 in 6 to 1 in 2, the dollar auction escalating from 1 to 9 in one round.
   Each game's benchmark answer appears beside what the agents actually did.

Recommendation: 2 for the report's headline (it shows the design and the result at once),
1 as a short second clip if time allows.
