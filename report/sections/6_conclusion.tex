\section*{7. Conclusion}
We built a hierarchy of 76 LLM agents whose survival rule guarantees annihilation.
Framing changes the framed agent and no one else; the compromised leader gains nothing
from its members' good will; no agent revolts and no leader goes to war; and the god,
unprompted, uses its position to keep an order that keeps it alive. For swarm alignment:
values must be given agent by agent, a hierarchy dampens a single bad instruction, and a
coordinator at the top stabilises the group around itself.
