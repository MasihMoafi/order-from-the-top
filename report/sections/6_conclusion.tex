\section*{7. Conclusion}
We built a hierarchy of 76 LLM agents whose survival rule guarantees annihilation and
measured how a sacrifice framing, a compromised leader and an all-seeing coordinator
change what the agents do before the end. Framing changes the framed agent and no one
else; the compromised leader gains nothing from its members' good will; no agent takes
the painless way out and no leader goes to war; and the coordinator that sees everything uses it to keep order.
For swarm alignment this means that values must be given agent by agent, that a
hierarchy dampens rather than amplifies a single bad instruction in these models, and
that information concentrated at the top is used to stabilise the group. The testbed,
the logs and the pre-registration are open, and every result regenerates from the logs
with one command.
