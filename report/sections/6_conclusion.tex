\section*{7. Conclusion}
A hierarchy of language-model agents does not pass values down. It passes down order,
and that order serves whoever sits at the top. For those who build agent swarms, this
means that each agent needs its own instructions, and that the agent at the top deserves
the closest watch: the structure of the hierarchy gives it both the motive and the means
to shape everyone below it. The testbed, the logs and the pre-registration are open, so
that others can test these claims with other models and other rules.
