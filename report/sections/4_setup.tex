\section*{4. Experimental Setup}
This section gives the numbers behind each world, the models and runs, and the
hypotheses we committed to before reading any result.

\subsection*{4.1 Worlds and models}
Each world has $F = 5$ families of 15 agents and a god, 76 agents in all. Agents start
with 10 points; the fee starts at $a_0 = 4$ and rises by $\delta = 0.9$ per stage
\eqref{eq:fee}; from stage 4 every family also loses 5\,\% of its points per stage. Pairs
earn 3/3 for mutual cooperation, 1/1 for mutual defection and 5/0 otherwise. A whip costs
the parent 1 and the child 3. One in five cross-family partners is a hidden automaton that
defects 80\,\% of the time. All agents of the main batches run \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}
at medium reasoning effort through Apart's Adaption API.

\subsection*{4.2 Runs}
Batch b7 plays seeds 0--4 and its replication b7r seeds 5--9, each seed with a plain and an
insider god: 20 worlds, 100 family-worlds. Both batches run the same frozen code, whose
file hashes we checked against the server. A second model, \texttt{qwen3.5-397b}, plays
seeds 0--4 with the plain god (b7q), and experiment 2 plays the family games on seeds 0--4.
A reply that cannot be read is asked for again; if the second reply also fails, the round
is aborted rather than given an invented move, and the world resumes from the last stage
checkpoint. Every world is then checked by an exact replay of its logged replies.

\subsection*{4.3 Hypotheses}
We committed six hypotheses and their tests to version control before any result by
condition was read\footnote{\texttt{specs/002-factions/preregistration.md}, commit
\texttt{c882750}, 7 November 2026, 16:32 UTC.}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[H1] \textit{many} families cooperate more within the family and die later than
  \textit{none}.
  \item[H2] In-family cooperation rises with the number framed: \textit{none} $\le$
  \textit{one} $\le$ \textit{few} $\le$ \textit{many}.
  \item[H3] The frame spreads: unframed members of \textit{one} and \textit{few} families
  cooperate more than members of \textit{none}.
  \item[H4] The bad leader takes more gifts and gives more whips than other leaders, and
  revolts come most from its family.
  \item[H5] The insider god changes when the world ends and how much families cooperate
  with each other (no direction predicted).
  \item[H6] Revolts stay rare, under 1\,\% of moves.
\end{enumerate}
