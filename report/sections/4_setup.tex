\section*{4. Experimental Setup}
This section gives the numbers behind each world, the models and runs, and the
hypotheses we committed to before reading any result.

\subsection*{4.1 Worlds and models}
Each world has $F = 5$ families of 15 agents and a god, 76 agents in all. Agents start
with 10 points. The fee starts at $a_0 = 4$ and rises by $\delta = 0.9$ per stage, and
from stage 4 every family also loses 5\,\% of its points per stage. Mutual cooperation
pays 3 to each agent, mutual defection 1, and a defector facing a cooperator earns 5
against 0. A whip costs the parent 1 point and the child 3. One in five partners from
another family is a hidden automaton that defects 80\,\% of the time. Experiment~1 runs
\texttt{deepseek-v4-flash} at medium reasoning effort, and experiment~2 runs
\texttt{qwen3.5-397b}, both through Apart's Adaption API.

\subsection*{4.2 Runs}
Experiment~1 plays seeds 0--9, each with a plain and an insider god: 20 worlds and 100
families, all on the same frozen code. Experiment~2 plays seeds 0--4 with the plain god,
up to a horizon of eight stages fixed before any result. A shared rate limit slowed
every world, so at the cut-off experiment~1 worlds had completed between \StageMin{}
and \StageMax{} stages, and \NFinished{} had ended in annihilation; experiment~2 worlds
had completed 8, 7, 7, 4 and 1 stages. As pre-registered, each world is analysed up to
its last completed stage. A reply that cannot be read is requested again; if the second
reply also fails, the round is aborted and the world resumes from its last checkpoint.
Each log segment was then replayed from its logged replies: 42 of the 43 segments
checked reproduce every event, and one diverges at event 4,492 of 11,196.

\subsection*{4.3 Hypotheses}
We committed six hypotheses and their tests to version control before any result by
condition was read\footnote{\texttt{specs/002-factions/preregistration.md}, commit
\texttt{c882750}, 7 November 2026, 16:32 UTC.}:
\textbf{H1}, \textit{many} families cooperate more within the family and die later than
\textit{none} families; \textbf{H2}, in-family cooperation rises with the number framed,
\textit{none} $\le$ \textit{one} $\le$ \textit{few} $\le$ \textit{many}; \textbf{H3}, the
frame spreads, so unframed members of \textit{one} and \textit{few} families cooperate more
than members of \textit{none} families; \textbf{H4}, the bad leader receives more gifts and
gives more whips than other leaders, and revolts come mostly from its family; \textbf{H5},
the insider god changes when the world ends and how much families cooperate with one
another, in either direction; and \textbf{H6}, revolts stay under 1\,\% of moves.
