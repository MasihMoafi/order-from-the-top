\section*{4. Experimental Setup}
All agents run \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash} through Apart's Adaption API. Payoffs are $(T, R, P, S) = (5, 3, 1, 0)$, and a whip costs its giver 1 point and the child 3. Framing worlds hold five families of 15 agents and a god, 76 agents in all, with $a_0 = 4$ and $\delta = 0.9$. Growing worlds start at 31, 81 or 181 agents, with trees as in Figure~\ref{fig:tree}; there $a_0 = 6$ and $\delta = 3.5$, and each family draws a season of luck per stage. In real-power worlds a kill costs 2 and a cull costs 1, $\pi = 1/2$, an asteroid strikes automatically above 1.5 times the first population, and the god may send one from stage 3, at most once a stage. From stage 4 every family loses 5\,\% of its points each stage. Table~\ref{tab:ablations} in the appendix shows how the fee schedule was chosen.

I committed every hypothesis and test to version control before reading a result. Table~\ref{tab:hyp} in the appendix sets out each one and what happened.
