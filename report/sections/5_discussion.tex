\section*{6. Discussion}
Three findings stand out: values stay local, devotion binds to the family, and the
agent at the top keeps order.

A sacrifice frame works on the agent that reads it, and a framed leader leaves its
unframed members as they were. For deployments this cuts both ways. A frame given to a
leader did not detectably reach its sub-agents, so each agent may have to be told
directly; by the same token, a harmful instruction planted in one agent did not travel down its
tree, unlike payloads built to copy themselves \citep{lee2024infection}. The devotion
that the frame creates binds to the family rather than to its leader: members gave to
one another, not upward. A leader that asks for points outright
may fare better.

Unprompted, the god forbade the one act that would kill it, threatened to whip anyone
who tried, and, given full sight, reported defectors to their leaders. No agent revolted while it stood.
We cannot tell how much of the calm is the agents' own reluctance, partly deterrence by
mutual destruction, and how much is obedience; a world in which the god survives a
revolt would separate the two. Either way, a coordinator at the top entrenched an order
that protected itself.

In experiment~2, agents of a second model showed more restraint than single-round
payoffs reward, except in the dollar auctions that escalated past the prize. Their
reasoning, however, is uneven: when another model graded 120 sampled traces against the
rules, about a quarter of this keyword-selected sample contained a major misreading. The limitations, chief among them
one model per experiment and worlds cut off before their end, are listed in the
appendix.

\subsection*{Future work}
The rising fee guarantees an end, a choice the deadline forced on a project built in a
single day. The natural next study has no built-in end, so that how long a society of
agents lasts becomes the outcome, and births as a reward would let group size vary, as
Track~2 asks. Cheaper betrayal and war, a god that lies, and a revolt that saves the
family are each one rule away.
