\section*{Appendix: Ablations}
Two constants govern how long a world lasts. Table~\ref{tab:ablations} shows how the fee of Equation~\ref{eq:fee} behaves for scripted players with no language
model, over three seeds and a cap of 16 stages. I chose the framing fee $a_0 = 4$, $\delta = 0.9$ because under it a family that always cooperates can last, losing most but not all of its members,
while one that always defects is gone within a few stages. Lower fees leave cooperation with little to decide; higher fees end the world regardless.

\toprule

\midrule

3 & 0.9 & 16.0 & 55.7 & 4.0 \\

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
