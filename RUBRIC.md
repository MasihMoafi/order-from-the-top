# Judging rubric (Swarm Alignment Hackathon, Apart Research, 6–8 Nov 2026)

Source: https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#guidelines (read 6 Nov 2026). Paraphrased; quotes marked.

Three dimensions, each scored 1–5. No weights stated. All tracks use the same rubric.

## 1. Impact Potential & Innovation
How much would it matter if it worked, and how new is it? "For scores of 4-5: is this actually new to the field, or replicating recent work?"
- 1: no clear problem or no novelty.
- 2: real problem, generic or well-trodden approach.
- 3: clear problem, reasonable approach, some novelty in framing or method.
- 4: important problem with an original approach, or a neglected area; others could build on it.
- 5: critical problem, genuinely novel approach or new direction, clear theory of change.

## 2. Execution Quality
How sound are method, implementation and findings?
- 1: broken method, uninterpretable results.
- 2: significant gaps (missing validation, flawed design, incomplete).
- 3: technically solid for the timeframe; interpretable results; limitations stated.
- 4: thorough method, convincing validation; results support the conclusions.
- 5: ambitious scope done rigorously; surprising findings, novel methods or unusually robust validation.

## 3. Presentation & Clarity
- 1: cannot tell what is claimed.
- 2: key information buried or missing, or too long.
- 3: problem, method, findings and limitations understandable without undue effort.
- 4: well structured, quickly understood.
- 5: a model for presenting this kind of work.

## Rules that can disqualify or cost points
- Made-up results or references: disqualification.
- Pre-sprint code not marked "preexisting" in README and report: disqualification risk; only weekend work is judged.
- A judge may decline to score a report too unclear to follow.
- Report 4–8 pages (hard cap, excluding references and appendices), official template, abstract 150–250 words, required appendix "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations".
- Max two projects per person. Deadline Sun 8 Nov 23:59 AoE (Mon 9 Nov 11:59 UTC).
